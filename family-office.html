Family Office Services

Complex wealth requires a single point of clarity.

We act as the central coordination layer for clients whose holdings span traditional structures and digital asset ecosystems — aligning trusteeship, corporate governance, custodial arrangements, and advisor relationships so that decisions remain consistent, documented, and in control regardless of how many jurisdictions or asset classes are involved.

What We Do

  • Single coordination point across trustees, advisors, and custodians

  • Family council structures and long-term governance protocols

FAQ

What does a Liechtenstein trustee do in a family office structure?

+

A Liechtenstein trustee acts as the legally accountable steward of the structure — holding assets, enforcing governance rules, coordinating advisors, and ensuring continuity. In a family office context, we extend this role to cover reporting, decision documentation, and long-term family governance.

Can a family office structure manage both traditional and digital assets?

+

Yes. We work with structures that combine conventional assets — real estate, equity stakes, private equity — with digital assets such as cryptocurrency portfolios, DeFi positions, and tokenised holdings. Unified governance and consolidated reporting is applied across all asset classes.

How do you handle families based in multiple countries?

+

Our team works across Liechtenstein, Austria, and Poland and advises on structures that span multiple jurisdictions. We coordinate with local advisors and ensure that the overarching governance structure remains coherent regardless of where family members live or where assets are held.
