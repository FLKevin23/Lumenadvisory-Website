About

Located in the heart of Liechtenstein, Lumen Advisory brings clarity to complexity.

We partner with internationally minded entrepreneurs, founders and families who value structure, discretion and long-term thinking.

As an independent Liechtenstein trustee company, we combine fiduciary expertise with legal and notarial experience across multiple jurisdictions. Our work spans asset structuring, succession planning, relocation and long-term family governance.

Led by experienced professionals with international backgrounds, we operate with direct involvement and personal accountability in every mandate. Our offices in Liechtenstein, Vienna and Kraków reflect the cross-border perspective in which our clients live and operate.

With deep fiduciary expertise and clear direction, we enable confident, forward-looking action today and across generations.
