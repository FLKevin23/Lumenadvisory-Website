Accounting & Reporting

Accounting within fiduciary structures is not just about numbers—it is about control and traceability. For clients with digital asset holdings, we provide reporting that covers on-chain and off-chain activity—including DeFi positions, staking rewards, NFT transactions, and token allocations—alongside conventional assets.

A common scenario involves a Liechtenstein foundation holding both traditional shareholdings and a managed crypto portfolio, requiring consolidated reporting that satisfies trustee oversight, regulatory scrutiny, and the specific accounting treatment applicable to digital asset classes across multiple jurisdictions.

What We Do

  • Consolidated reporting across multiple jurisdictions and asset classes
  • On-chain and off-chain financial statements
  • Regulatory compliance reporting for fiduciary structures
  • Audit-ready bookkeeping for foundations, trusts, and holding companies

Who This Is For

Clients whose asset structures span multiple countries and include both conventional and digital assets — and who need coherent, accurate, and regulation-ready financial oversight from a single provider.

FAQ

What does accounting for a Liechtenstein foundation involve?

Liechtenstein foundations are required to maintain proper accounting records and, depending on their size and activities, may be subject to audit requirements. We prepare annual accounts, manage bookkeeping, and ensure that the foundation's financial records are accurate, compliant, and suitable for trustee oversight.

How do you handle reporting for crypto holdings and DeFi positions?

We treat digital asset holdings as part of the consolidated financial picture. This includes recording on-chain transactions, valuing cryptocurrency positions, accounting for DeFi yields and staking rewards, and applying appropriate accounting treatment across jurisdictions. Reporting is tailored to the applicable regulatory and trustee requirements.

Do you provide audit support?

Yes. We prepare accounts and supporting documentation to a standard that supports third-party audit. Where audit is required — either by regulation or by client preference — we coordinate with the appointed auditor and provide full documentation of the accounting basis used.
