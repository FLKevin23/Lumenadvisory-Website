FAQ

Why do I need a notary for a Liechtenstein foundation?

+

Under Liechtenstein law, the establishment of certain legal entities — including foundations — requires notarial execution of the founding documents. A notary verifies the identity of the parties, certifies the legal validity of the documents, and ensures compliance with formal requirements. Dr. Tomasz Klimek is a licensed notary in Liechtenstein.

Can you execute documents across multiple jurisdictions?

+

Yes. Our team is admitted to practice in Liechtenstein, Austria, and Poland. For cross-border matters involving other jurisdictions, we coordinate with trusted local counsel. We are experienced in managing the legal formalities required when documents must be valid in more than one country.

What is the role of a Liechtenstein attorney in a foundation structure?

+

A Liechtenstein attorney ensures that the foundation's statutes, governance documents, and operational decisions remain legally sound and enforceable. This includes reviewing beneficiary arrangements, advising on regulatory obligations, and supervising any structural changes over time.
