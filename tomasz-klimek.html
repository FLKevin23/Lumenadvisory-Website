Dr. Tomasz Klimek
Attorney-at-Law (FL, A & PL) | Notary Public | Partner

Dr. Tomasz Klimek is a Public Notary and Attorney-at-Law in Liechtenstein and a partner at Mairhofer Advokatur. He advises on and oversees legally complex fiduciary and corporate structures, with a particular focus on cross-border foundations, holding entities, and asset-holding arrangements involving Liechtenstein, Austria, and Poland.

Dr. Klimek holds a Doctor of Laws from the University of Vienna and multiple LL.M. degrees in company, foundation, and trust law. He is admitted to the bar in Liechtenstein, Austria, and Poland and is the only Polish-speaking attorney in Liechtenstein, allowing him to bridge jurisdictions with precision and accountability.
