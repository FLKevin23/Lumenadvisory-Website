Succession Planning

Succession planning is rarely about a single transfer; it is about control, protection, and continuity over many years. We structure succession solutions that cover both conventional and digital wealth—from business interests and real estate to crypto holdings and NFT collections—remaining legally robust and enforceable across jurisdictions.

A typical mandate may involve a technology founder transferring equity, a cryptocurrency portfolio, and digital art holdings into a Liechtenstein foundation—with clear succession rules covering private key access, custodial arrangements, and wallet governance—ensuring nothing is lost or frozen at the moment of transition.

What We Do

  • Transfer of business interests into foundations or trust structures
  • Succession rules covering digital assets and private key governance
  • Multi-generational continuity and family governance planning
  • Protection against forced heirship and cross-border legal conflicts

Who This Is For

Business owners, founders, and families who want to ensure their wealth — including unconventional or digital assets — transfers according to their wishes, with no ambiguity, no court battles, and no frozen accounts.

FAQ

Can a Liechtenstein foundation protect assets from forced heirship rules?

Liechtenstein foundations can provide significant protection against forced heirship claims under foreign law, depending on the applicable jurisdiction and the structure of the foundation. This is a fact-specific analysis and we assess each client's situation individually before recommending a structure.

What happens to crypto and digital assets when there is no succession plan?

Without proper planning, digital assets can become inaccessible or lost entirely. Private keys may be unknown to heirs, wallet access may be tied to single devices, and legal frameworks for claiming digital assets vary widely. We build succession structures that specifically address key access, custodial arrangements, and wallet governance.

When is the right time to start succession planning?

Earlier than most people expect. Succession planning is most effective when designed alongside the asset structure itself — not as an afterthought when assets are already scattered. If you have meaningful wealth across more than one country, the time to plan is now.
