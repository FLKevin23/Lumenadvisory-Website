Who We Are

Lumen Advisory is an independent trustee company based in Liechtenstein. We are not a bank, and we do not manage investments — we build and maintain the legal architecture around what our clients have built: foundations, trusts and companies designed to protect, structure and outlast.

Our work spans asset protection, succession planning, family office coordination, relocation, philanthropy, accounting and legal & notarial services — for entrepreneurs, founders and families operating across multiple jurisdictions, with a particular focus on Central and Eastern Europe.

From offices in Schaan, Vienna and Kraków, we work where our clients live.

Led By Remo Mairhofer

Remo Mairhofer is the founder and CEO of Lumen Advisory — an Attorney-at-Law admitted to the Liechtenstein Bar, a licensed trustee, and a Substitute Judge at the Constitutional Court of Liechtenstein.

He holds an LL.M. from the University of Sydney and two master's degrees from the University of St. Gallen (HSG), in Law and in Banking & Finance, and has completed the Liechtenstein trustee examinations.
