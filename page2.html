Our Services

Our Services

Family Office

We act as a central point of coordination where family structures become complex and scattered across borders.

In practice, this can involve a family with operating companies in Austria, investment assets held through a Liechtenstein foundation, and family members living between Switzerland and Singapore.

Succession Planning

Succession planning is rarely about a single transfer; it is about control, protection, and continuity over many years.

A typical mandate may involve a German entrepreneur transferring business interests into a Liechtenstein foundation.

Relocation Services

Relocation affects more than residency—it often changes how existing structures are viewed by authorities.

We guide clients through relocations by adjusting legal and fiduciary frameworks early.

Philanthropy

Philanthropy works best when generosity is paired with structure.

This may include setting up a Liechtenstein charitable foundation that funds educational or social projects abroad.

Accounting

Accounting within fiduciary structures is not just about numbers—it is about control and traceability.

A common scenario involves a Liechtenstein foundation holding participations in operating companies in Austria and Poland.

Legal Advice & Notarial Services

Legal advice and notarial services are fully integrated into our fiduciary work.

This often includes the notarial establishment of foundations or companies and ongoing legal supervision.
