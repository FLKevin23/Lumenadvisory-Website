The people behind Lumen Advisory

Remo Mairhofer
Attorney-at-Law | Trustee | Founder & CEO

Remo Mairhofer is an Attorney-at-Law and licensed trustee, serving as Founder and CEO of LUMEN ADVISORY Trust reg. and founder of Mairhofer Advokatur in Liechtenstein.

He holds degrees in Law and Banking & Finance from the University of St. Gallen and an LL.M. from the University of Sydney.

Dr. Tomasz Klimek
Attorney-at-Law (FL, A & PL) | Notary Public | Partner

Dr. Tomasz Klimek is a Public Notary and Attorney-at-Law in Liechtenstein and a partner at Mairhofer Advokatur.

Dr. Klimek holds a Doctor of Laws from the University of Vienna and is the only Polish-speaking attorney in Liechtenstein.
